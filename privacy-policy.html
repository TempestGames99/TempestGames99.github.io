Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Applies to every game by Mozma Games

Mozma Games ("we", "us") makes games for mobile app stores and for web game sites. This policy covers all of our games and apps ("our games"), wherever you play them. Where a single game does something different, it is listed under Game-specific notes.

In short
  • We do not collect your name, email or other personal information. Our games have no accounts and no servers of ours.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device.
  • Some games send anonymous gameplay statistics (such as which levels are reached) to an analytics service, so we can see where players get stuck and improve the game.
  • Ads in our games are provided by third parties (such as Google AdMob, or the website you play on), and they may collect data as described below.

1. Information we collect

None that identifies you by name. Game progress, settings and in-game items are stored locally on your device or in your browser's storage, and that save data stays on your device.

Games that use analytics send anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider, as described in Analytics. We do not sell any data, and we do not combine it with information from other sources to identify you.

2. Advertising

Mobile apps (Google Play and other app stores)

Our mobile apps show ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data.

Google processes this information under its own policies: see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Web versions on game websites

When you play one of our games on a game website (for example CrazyGames, Poki, Y8, GameDistribution, GamePix or GameMonetize), that website serves the game and any ads around or inside it, and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy. Please read the privacy policy of the website you are playing on.

3. Analytics

Some of our games (listed under Game-specific notes) use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) to understand how the game is played: how far players progress, where they stop, and how the game performs. It may collect:

  • a random identifier created for the game on your device (not your name or email),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • your approximate country, derived from your IP address,
  • session start and length, and gameplay events such as levels or goals reached, in-game currency earned and spent, and purchases made,
  • error and performance reports.

We see this information as totals and trends, not as a profile of you. GameAnalytics processes it under its own privacy policy.

Some of our games (listed under Game-specific notes) are made with Unity and use Unity's in-app purchasing and analytics services (Unity Technologies). When you view or make a purchase, Unity's purchasing software reports that step and the item involved to Unity, together with device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version) and identifiers (such as an identifier Unity creates for the installation and, where available, your device's advertising ID). Unity uses this to run and improve its services, under its own privacy policy.

4. In-app purchases

Where a game sells optional items, purchases are processed by the app store (for example Google Play Billing). Your payment details stay with the store; the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. In games made with Unity, Unity's purchasing software also reports purchase events to Unity (see Analytics).

5. Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), our games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. In our mobile apps you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options. Where consent is required for analytics, it is asked for in the same way.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting your data. Use the in-game reset where a game has one (for example Settings → Reset Everything), uninstall the app, or clear your browser's site data. This removes all game data from your device.

6. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security

Data sent by advertising and analytics partners' software (such as the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the GameAnalytics SDK and Unity's purchasing and analytics services) is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. The latest version always applies to all of our games.

9. Game-specific notes

Hazard Pay

Android app: Google AdMob interstitial, rewarded (always optional and labelled "Watch ad") and app-open ads, with Google's consent form where required. Web versions: ads, if any, are provided by the game website. The game itself collects nothing.

Wobble Shot League

Android app: Google AdMob interstitial, rewarded (always optional and labelled "Watch ad"), app-open and banner (lobby only) ads, with Google's consent form where required and Settings → Privacy Options to change it. Optional in-app purchases (coins, skins, shapes, removing ads) are processed by Google Play Billing: payment details stay with Google, and the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. Web versions: ads, if any, are provided by the game website. The game itself collects nothing.

Rise & Bake

Android app: Google AdMob rewarded ads (always optional and labelled "Watch ad"), interstitial ads at natural breaks (no more than one every few minutes) and app-open ads when you come back to the game, with Google's consent form where required and Pause → About & privacy → Ad privacy choices to change it. Optional in-app purchases (coins, a starter bundle, helpers, a flask of tea for Uncle Thomson, golden oven mitts, removing ads) are processed by Google Play Billing: payment details stay with Google, and the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. Uses GameAnalytics to see how players progress through the bakery (store levels, days played, things unlocked, guide steps reached, sessions, rewarded offers taken and purchases made), and Unity's in-app purchasing and analytics services, which report purchase events with device information and identifiers to Unity. Web versions: ads, if any, are provided by the game website; no analytics. Progress and settings are saved on your device or in your browser.

Crash Courier

Android app (the update that replaced CarDash on Google Play): Google AdMob rewarded ads (always optional and labelled "Watch ad", to double a run's coins or for free coins in the shop), interstitial ads between runs only (at most one a minute, and none in a player's first runs) and app-open ads when you come back to the game, with Google's consent form where required and Menu → Privacy Options to change it. Optional in-app purchases (coins, premium vehicle skins, a welcome pack, removing ads) are processed by Google Play Billing: payment details stay with Google, and the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. Uses GameAnalytics to see how players progress through the game (roads started, finished or crashed, towns reached, first-time steps, coins earned and spent, ads shown and purchases made), and Unity's in-app purchasing services, which report purchase events with device information and identifiers to Unity. Progress and settings are saved on your device; for players of the earlier CarDash, the coins CarDash saved on the device are carried into Crash Courier once.

10. This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and game trailers are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode; like any website you visit, those services receive your IP address and browser details when they send their content. Links to app stores take you to that store, under its own privacy policy.
