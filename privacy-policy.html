Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Applies to every game by Tempest Games

Tempest Games ("we", "us") makes games for mobile app stores and for web game sites. This policy covers all of our games and apps ("our games"), wherever you play them. Where a single game does something different, it is listed under Game-specific notes.

In short
  • We do not collect, receive or store your personal information. Our games have no accounts and no servers of ours.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device and are never sent to us.
  • Ads in our games are provided by third parties (such as Google AdMob, or the website you play on), and they may collect data as described below.

1. Information we collect

None that identifies you. Game progress, settings and in-game items are stored locally on your device or in your browser's storage. This data stays on your device; we never receive it.

We do not run analytics services of our own, and we do not sell or share any data, because we hold none.

2. Advertising

Mobile apps (Google Play and other app stores)

Our mobile apps show ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data.

Google processes this information under its own policies: see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Web versions on game websites

When you play one of our games on a game website (for example CrazyGames, Poki, Y8, GameDistribution, GamePix or GameMonetize), that website serves the game and any ads around or inside it, and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy. Please read the privacy policy of the website you are playing on.

3. Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), our games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. In our mobile apps you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting your data. Use the in-game reset where a game has one (for example Settings → Reset Everything), uninstall the app, or clear your browser's site data. This removes all game data from your device.

4. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security

Data sent by advertising partners' software (such as the Google Mobile Ads SDK) is encrypted in transit.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. The latest version always applies to all of our games.

7. Game-specific notes

Hazard Pay

Android app: Google AdMob interstitial, rewarded (always optional and labelled "Watch ad") and app-open ads, with Google's consent form where required. Web versions: ads, if any, are provided by the game website. The game itself collects nothing.

Wobble Shot League

Android app: Google AdMob interstitial, rewarded (always optional and labelled "Watch ad"), app-open and banner (lobby only) ads, with Google's consent form where required and Settings → Privacy Options to change it. Optional in-app purchases (coins, skins, shapes, removing ads) are processed by Google Play Billing: payment details stay with Google, and the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. Web versions: ads, if any, are provided by the game website. The game itself collects nothing.
