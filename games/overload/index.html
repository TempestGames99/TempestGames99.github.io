About the game

Aim. Fire. Merge!

A hologram reactor floats above its stage, and you're the one feeding it.

Drag sideways to drive the heavy cannon round its rail, drag up or down to aim, lift your finger to fire. Inside the reactor there's no gravity: cubes drift, tumble and bounce off the containment field. Hit a cube with the same number and the two fuse into the next, from 2 and 4 into the thousands, and the new cube leaps at its own match to keep the chain going. Every cube adds to the reactor's load, and you can watch it charge up as it fills. Let it sit full too long and it goes up with a bang.

  • Chain reactionsEvery merge leaps to the next match: one good shot can set off a whole chain.
  • A reactor that fights backIt glows, crackles and shakes as it fills, until it overloads.
  • Reactor abilitiesShockwave, Stasis and the Recycler, charged by your merges.
  • Upgrade cardsPick a new power at every score milestone: every run plays differently.
  • Three ways to playEndless Reactor, a new Daily Experiment each day and 24 Precision Trials.
  • The WorkshopPermanent LAB upgrades, cube styles and reactor skins to unlock.
