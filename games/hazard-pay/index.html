About the game

The sign says safe. The floor disagrees.

Welcome to the worst job in the world: a factory where every room is out to get you.

Floors vanish, doors run away, and spikes pop up exactly where you were about to land. Tap in and you're on your first shift straight away. Every trap has a rule you can learn, every death is a punchline, and the next try is always half a second away.

Start as Cinder, the fire salamander who wall-jumps. Pick your worker after your first shifts, then recruit the rest of the crew: Pip the crash-test dummy double-jumps, and Rivet the robot safety inspector hovers and scans for hidden traps. Every coin you carry out of a shift is your hazard pay, to spend in the Locker.

  • 200 shifts20 chapters, from gentle lies to rooms that throw everything at you at once.
  • Three workersWall jump, double jump or hover: each one plays differently.
  • Traps that lieFake floors, runaway doors and spikes with perfect timing.
  • A skull for every deathWatch the pile grow at the trap that keeps getting you.
  • Daily rewardsA box every day, three daily missions, and Overtime: three new shifts every day.
  • The LockerHats, skins, trails and death bursts, bought with your hazard pay.
  • Hidden blueprintsSecrets only each worker can reach, for alternate colours.
  • Plays offlineSimple touch controls: move on the left, jump on the right.
