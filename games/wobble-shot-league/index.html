About the game

Last shape standing

You're a chunky shape with a gun in your hands, and 99 rivals want the same island.

Jump out of the drop plane, open your parachute and land where you like. Loot guns, armour and medkits, hop into a hover buggy to beat the closing circle, and fight your way to the last spot. Every gun is found inside the match, so every round starts fair.

Not in the mood for a full royale? Hold out against endless Survival waves, pick an upgrade after each one and take on a King boss every fifth wave, or jump into a fast 8 v 8 Team Deathmatch. Every match is against bots, so it plays offline with no waiting.

  • 100-shape battle royalePlane drop, parachutes and hover buggies, in Solo, Duos or Squads.
  • Endless SurvivalWaves of shapes, a new perk after each, and King boss fights.
  • 8 v 8 Team DeathmatchPlus quick 16-shape Arenas for a fast fight.
  • A full arsenalPistols, SMGs, rifles, shotguns, snipers, rocket launchers and grenades.
  • 10 shapesSquare, Ball, Barrel, Egg and more, each with its own health and special move.
  • Gun skinsA look for every weapon, from Arctic to Diamond.
  • Aim that helpsAim assist lands your shots without ever taking control away.
  • Plays offlineTouch controls made for phones: move stick, drag to aim, big fire button.
