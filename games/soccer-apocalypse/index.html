About the game

Kick zombies off the pitch!

The city has fallen to the undead, but the football never stopped.

Lead a team of big-headed survivors into ruined stadiums and play fast 3D matches against zombie sides. Run onto the ball, leap for headers, slide in for tackles and bend shots into the corners. A few seconds in, zombies start climbing over the barriers to come for you. Blast the ball through them to knock them flat, fill your super meter and win back the city one district at a time, all the way to the bosses that rule each stadium.

  • Football in 3DKicks, headers, chips, volleys and slide tackles, with easy touch controls.
  • Zombies on the pitchThey climb the walls mid-match. Knock them down with the ball.
  • Six wild superpowersFireball, Giant Head, Deep Freeze, Turbo Sprint, Ball Magnet and Shockwave.
  • Take back the cityFour districts, each with its own stadium and a boss to beat.
  • Penalties and goalkeepingShoot-outs, set pieces, and your turn between the posts.
  • Nine survivorsCollect players, kits, boots and balls, and upgrade your team and base.
