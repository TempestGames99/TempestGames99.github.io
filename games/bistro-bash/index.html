About the game

Cook for monsters!

You're the skeleton chef of a graveyard bistro, and tonight the whole spooky town is hungry.

Walk your kitchen with one thumb and work it with the other: take ingredients from the crate, chop them on the block, stir the cauldron, flip burgers in the skillet and bake pies in the bone oven, then hand each dish across the counter, or flick it straight at the customer. Every monster has a mood. The Impatient Count won't wait, the Bottomless Zombie keeps ordering, the Full-Moon Wolf smashes your kitchen if you ignore him, and the Pumpkin King expects a three-course feast. Each new recipe comes with a quick how-to, so you learn one dish at a time and then master the rush.

  • Monster customersEight regulars and a boss, each with their own mood and quirks.
  • Spooky recipesEyeball Broth, Pumpkin Ghoul Burgers, Batwing Pie, Witchlight Tea and more.
  • 18 story nightsSix chapters, from a graveyard shack to the Pumpkin King's court.
  • Grow your kitchenLay out your stations, expand to the Necro-Citadel and upgrade every one.
  • More ways to playEndless Rush, a Daily Special and the Halloween Feast event.
  • Dress the partChef outfits, serving trails, cauldron skins and kitchen themes.
