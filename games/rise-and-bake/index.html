About the game

The ovens are warm again

Grandma's bakery on Maple Lane has stood shut for years. With your savings in your pocket and Uncle Thomson at your side, it's time to open the doors again.

Rise & Bake is a cozy first-person bakery sim with nothing on a timer. Tip the flour in, knead the dough, shape every croissant onto the tray and slide it into the oven. Fill the shelves, flip the sign to OPEN and serve your neighbours at the till, one bag at a time.

As word gets around, the shop grows: new recipes, new fittings, a coffee corner, tables for dine-in guests and a team of helpers, until the bakery hums along while you paint the walls and pick the next treat to learn.

  • Bake by handTip in each ingredient, knead, and shape every piece onto the tray yourself.
  • Never rushedNo timers. Customers wait patiently and nothing in the oven ever burns.
  • No menus to dig throughEverything happens in the world: walk up to it and use it.
  • Run the tillBag each order, take cash or card, and hand back the right change.
  • Make it yoursBuy ovens, displays, tables and a coffee corner, then paint and rearrange.
  • Uncle ThomsonYour mentor walks you through the first day and shows you what's next whenever you ask.
  • Grow and hireLevel up, learn new recipes and hire a baker, cashier, server and cleaner.
  • A town around youA busy high street, regulars at your tables, and a shop cat who'd like a snack.
