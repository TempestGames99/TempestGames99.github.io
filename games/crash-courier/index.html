About the game

Drift. Deliver. Don't drop it!

You're the courier with a stack of parcels on the roof and a van that loves to slide. The deeper the drift, the more you earn, until the stack tips.

Turn the wheel to throw the van into every bend, pull into each stop to park and unload, and pick up the next load at the parcel depot. At every town, bank your coins or drive on to the next road while the multiplier keeps climbing. Seven hand-made roads join six towns across Sunny County, from the harbour lighthouse to the observatory on the summit, and each road is the same every time: learn its bends, then master it for three stars.

  • Drift with a stackSlide the van for coins while the parcels on the roof wobble and tip.
  • Sunny County7 hand-made roads and 6 towns, from morning to night, each with its landmark.
  • Bank or drive onCash out at a town, or push on for a bigger multiplier and a bigger risk.
  • HazardsGold routes, barrels, collapsing slabs, narrow bridges and traffic.
  • 10 vehiclesVans and trucks to unlock, with paint jobs, parts and cargo skins.
  • Steer your wayA wheel, buttons, slide or tilt. A daily contract, and it plays offline.
